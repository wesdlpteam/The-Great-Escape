Task 2: The Lion's Leap (The Skip Counting Grid)

The Station Setup: Head over to the Numeracy Grid Zone. You will see a scattered field of Post-it notes stuck to the carpet, numbered clearly from 1 to 10 in a winding matrix layout across the floor.
  • The Mission: The lions need to slip through a high-frequency security grid that only shuts down on an even count. Place your Indi car directly on Post-it note number 2. You must arrange your silicone colour tiles on the floor to code a path that skip counts by 2s, navigating Indi sequentially from 2, to 4, to 6, and coming to a complete stop precisely on Post-it note number 8.
🐅 Guard Patrol Warning: The laser beams are sensitive to pacing! Indi is not allowed to just cruise between the targets; it must execute a mandatory 45-degree stunt (using an Orange or Teal tile) or a grand victory spin (using a Purple tile) precisely on top of, or directly beside, every number in your skip counting sequence (2, 4, 6, and 8) to disrupt the sensor beams.

🔮 Act 3: The Grand Finale

Task 1: The Great Habitat Run (Biological Sciences)

The Station Setup: Head over to the Biomes & Habitats Zone. On the floor, you will see three large destination zones marked clearly on large sheets of paper: The Ocean 🌊, The Rainforest 🌳, and The Desert 🌵. Nearby, you will find cards for three escaped circus animals: Seal 🦭, Monkey 🐒, and Camel 🐫.
  • The Mission: The final security gates are closing! To complete the breakout, your team cannot just save one animal—you must return all 3 animals to their correct geographic homes simultaneously to win. Place an Indi car on each of the three animal cards and code three distinct pathways across the floor to steer them all safely into their native habitat zones.
🐒 Guard Patrol Warning: Biome terrain is unpredictable, and the guards are closing in! Before each Indi car reaches the safety of its habitat zone, your code must include a mandatory environmental adaptation:
  • The Monkey must hit a Blue tile (Slow) right before entering the thick, dense Rainforest.
  • The Camel must hit an Orange tile (Fast) to blast across the open Desert sand.
  • The Seal must hit a Purple tile (Celebration Spin) just as it dives into the Ocean!
