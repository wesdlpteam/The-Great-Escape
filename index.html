🎪 Act 2: Center Ring Feature (Choreography Hooks)
🔒
3. Cracking the Password Code (Zero-Prep Literacy) +250 Claps

The escape gate is locked with a code. Grab 5 random items sitting in the room (like a notebook, a coffee mug, or a pen). On a piece of scrap paper or a sticky note, write a simple phonics sound, sight word, or letter on each item and scatter them on the floor. Program Indi to steer past the decoy sounds and come to a complete stop next to the true password word.

🎪 Ringmaster's Decree: The security locks require vocal authentication! As Indi rolls past, the co-drivers must deliver a live dramatic commentary out loud, reading or sounding out each checkpoint it passes to maintain stealth.

🔒
4. The Lion's Leap (Zero-Prep Numeracy) +250 Claps

The lions are mapping a winding path across the circus floor to build up momentum. Write the numbers 1 to 10 on separate post-it notes. Scatter them across the floor in a winding zig-zag or scattered matrix, keeping them in numerical order. Program Indi to start at post-it number 1, navigate the winding path in order, and come to a complete stop on post-it number 8.

🎪 Ringmaster's Decree: The lions love to show off for the crowd! Indi is not allowed to just cruise past the numbers; it must execute a 45-degree stunt (Orange/Teal tile) or a grand victory spin (Purple tile) precisely on top of or beside the even-numbered post-it notes along the route.

The animals need a massive, noisy distraction to draw guards away from the gates. Program Indi to successfully navigate a winding path of Post-it notes on the floor, ending with a direct impact into a giant wooden block tower to knock it down with a satisfying crash!

You must find a creative way to launch Indi screen-free, keep it moving along the path, and trigger the loud tower crash using only your remaining colors.

6. The Gate Timer (The Grand Escape) +400 Claps

You have made it to the final automated perimeter fence! Build a narrow escape tunnel out of your wooden blocks, leading out to freedom. The catch? The main security gate at the very end of the tunnel only stays open for a few seconds before slamming shut and locking forever.

You must program Indi to enter the tunnel at absolute maximum velocity (Green tile), but as it crosses that specific laser post-it, it must execute a mandatory stunt or spin (Purple or Orange tile) to disable the alarm, recover its footing, and blast through the final gate before the timer runs out!
